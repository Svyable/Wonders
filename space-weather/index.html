How to read this view

The measured wind and magnetic field arrive from spacecraft upstream of Earth at L1. We estimate travel time using 1.5 million km ÷ measured speed. For any chosen Earth timestamp, the displayed boundary is computed only from a sample old enough to have plausibly arrived. This is an approximation, not an operational space-weather forecast.

  • Magnetopause: Shue et al. (1997/1998) empirical pressure/Bz boundary; model geometry, not a direct global measurement.
  • Bow shock, field lines, plasma sheet: pedagogical schematic, not traced spacecraft fields or an MHD run.
  • Aurora: NOAA OVATION short-term model forecast, and shown only for the live window. This is not a current photograph.
  • Kp and Hp30: indices for preceding intervals; timestamp and staleness matter.
  • Fallback: if the NOAA model inputs are missing, geometry uses a quiet illustrative baseline and is explicitly marked as such.

Sources: NOAA solar wind · NOAA OVATION · GFZ indices · AEGIS / MIT · TERRA.
