# WonderTime evidence and reconstruction policy

1. **Excavated / observed:** extant structures, excavated remains, secure archaeological contexts or verified contemporary documents. Display what is actually supported.
2. **Historical reconstruction:** period-correct visualization grounded in scholarship but still interpretive. Display date bounds and confidence and provide a toggle to hide reconstructed features.
3. **Ancient account:** testimony by Herodotus, Strabo and other writers is evidence of a text and its historical reception, not automatically a surveyed building plan.
4. **Measured anomaly:** radar, conductivity, muography and other instrumental data measure signals interpreted under assumptions. A signal is not an excavated chamber, entrance or artifact.
5. **Published hypothesis:** an interpretation that can be evaluated, challenged and potentially falsified. Note methodology, independent replication and opposing evidence.
6. **Unsupported or retracted:** off by default in immersive rendering. Present when educationally relevant with a prominent caveat and a source for the claim/retraction.

**Two dates belong to different layers:** a monument's probable date of construction, and the date a scholar reported or studied it. In a 4D atlas, modern discoveries reveal data about old structures; they do not add those structures to the past. When evidence is weak or absent, show ambiguity rather than a plausible-looking invented interior.

**Hawara-specific:** the cult precinct and pyramid are archaeological topics, while 3,000-room descriptions are ancient literature. Publicly cited geophysics does not provide a validated room-by-room 3D mesh. See `research/hawara_research_pack.json` for survey notes and limits.
