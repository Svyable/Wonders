# Attributions and research links

- **Earth basemap artwork** in this package was procedurally stylized from coarse GSHHS coastlines via the installed Basemap geography toolkit. This is artistic geographic context, not a satellite image. Coastlines originate with the Global Self-consistent, Hierarchical, High-resolution Geography database (GSHHG), NOAA / Wessel & Smith: https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/pwessel/gshhg/
- **Site catalog:** every world destination carries a direct public research link, largely to a UNESCO World Heritage Centre property page. The site data itself contains original short summaries; external documents and images are not bundled.
- **Giza and Hawara:** original site application supplied in this conversation and preserved under `source/legacy_giza_hawara_v2.html`; research links in `research/`. The separate Hawara research pack is user-provided.
- **3D engine:** the embedded legacy Giza atlas may load Three.js from third-party CDNs; Three.js is MIT-licensed: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/master/LICENSE

Research links are pointers, not blanket licenses to redistribute linked content or third-party model assets.
