# Hawara Labyrinth — source guide and evidence boundaries

**Institutional and academic references**

- [UCL Digital Egypt: Hawara](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/hawara1/index.html) — cemetery sequences, temple/pyramid association.
- [UCL Digital Egypt: Amenemhat III](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/chronology/amenemhatIII.html) — dynasty, building activities, Hawara and Dahshur.
- [UCL Petrie Museum: Hawara mummy portraits](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/engage/museums-collections/petrie-museum-egyptian-and-sudanese-archaeology/top-objects-petrie-museum/hawara-mummy-portraits) — portraits, excavations 1888–1889 and 1910–1911.
- [UCL: Roman Hawara](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/hawara1/roman/index.html) — Roman burials and limitations of Petrie's records.
- [UCL: Roman tomb chapels](https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/hawara1/roman/stelae.html) — cemetery architecture.
- [UCL: A Virtual Exploration of the Lost Labyrinth](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1372/) — 2000 interpretive 3D modeling project; distinguishes evidence and conjecture.
- [Uytterhoeven & Blom-Böer, Journal of Egyptian Archaeology 88 (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1177/030751330208800108) — published field survey and Middle Kingdom sculptures.
- [University of York: LABYRINTH research project](https://pure.york.ac.uk/portal/en/projects/labyrinth-conservation-analysis-and-virtual-reconstruction-of-arc/) — heritage and groundwater conditions.
- [Oxford Griffith Institute: Hawara excavation archive](https://egyptartefacts.griffith.ox.ac.uk/node/1119/) — Petrie's seasons and publications.
- [Petrie at Hawara: Pioneering Debatable Standards?](https://doi.org/10.5334/bha-666) — modern critique of historical excavations.

**Ancient testimony**

- [Herodotus, *Histories* II.148](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Herodotus/2b%2A.html) — described 12 courts and 3,000 chambers, but said he had not seen the underground ones. The number is **an ancient author's report**, not a verified floor plan or archaeological measurement. The scene must **never** label specific subterranean rooms as surveyed on that basis.

**Unverified modern interpretation**

- [Carlotto, “Space-Based Ground Penetrating Radar Imaging of a Possible Underground Structure at Hawara” (2025)](https://doi.org/10.31275/20253579) — a hypothetical interpretation of remote-sensing responses; not an independently excavated or confirmed labyrinth. Presented as an unresolved claim, not as an archaeological discovery.

## Source and presentation boundaries

- There is **no authenticated detailed plan** of the purported labyrinth's thousands of rooms. The source ZIP's illustrative rectangles are symbolic markers for a temple zone; their boundaries, chambers, orientation and scale are not survey assertions.
- Hawara's construction and dynastic chronology are **approximate**. Roman portrait cemeteries were active over generations, not exactly on one date.
- Amenemhat III's Hawara pyramid is preserved as a **degraded mudbrick core**; the once-faced shape is a stylized interpretation.
- Old and newer archaeological findings are **not equivalent** to an animated staffing simulation. We do not assign made-up Hawara workforce numbers.
- Modern radar anomalies, speculative reconstructions and ancient travel reports are contextualized alongside excavated artifacts and publications rather than being merged into one confidence class.
