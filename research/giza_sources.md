## Editorial boundaries

- No evidence of an advanced ancient Giza civilization in 10,500 BCE.
- Fourth Dynasty reign dates differ among standard chronologies; the model uses a conventional approximate sequence.
- The 2026 retraction specifically concerns a 2022 satellite-radar study and must not be conflated with independent cosmic-muon measurements.
- Modern scientific results are dated by their reported findings; the underlying ancient structures already existed before their detection.
